FAQ

Good questions.

How long does a website take?

A landing page takes about a week and a business website 2–3 weeks. Web apps and custom software depend on scope, and we give you a clear timeline before starting.

Will I own the code and the website?

Yes. Once the project is paid for, the code, design and content are yours.

Do you offer support after launch?

Yes. Our Care Plans cover hosting, backups, small edits and uptime checks every month.

I'm not technical. Can you still help?

Absolutely. We explain everything in plain language and handle the technical decisions with you.

How does payment work?

Websites have a fixed quote agreed upfront. Larger web apps and software are paid in milestones, so you only pay as work is delivered.

Do you work with clients outside India?

Yes. We work remotely with clients anywhere and communicate over email, WhatsApp or video calls.
